2. What data is processed

2.1 Account data

When you create an account: your email address, a password (stored in encrypted form and never accessible to the publisher), and the display name you choose. If you use Google sign-in, your email address, name and Google identifier are passed to the Application by Google.

2.2 Game data

The information you enter: names of courses and layouts, pars, hole-by-hole scores, round dates, and the first names or nicknames of other players you add to a card.

When you enter a playing partner's name, you are processing personal data about them. We recommend using first names or nicknames only, and telling the people concerned.

2.3 Payment data

If you subscribe, payment is handled exclusively by Stripe. The publisher never has access to your card number, which does not pass through the Application. Stored in the Application's database are: a Stripe customer identifier, a subscription identifier, the subscription status and its renewal date.

2.4 Technical data and analytics

If, and only if, you consent, analytics data is collected through Google Analytics: pages viewed, visit duration, device type, operating system, language, and approximate location derived from the IP address (anonymisation enabled).

6. Your rights

Under the conditions set out in the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access (Art. 15): obtain a copy of your data.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): correct inaccurate data. Name, email address and password can be changed directly in the Application, under “My account”.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): request deletion of your account and data.
  • Restriction (Art. 18) and objection (Art. 21).
  • Portability (Art. 20): receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)) at any time for analytics, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Post-mortem directives: decide what happens to your data after your death (Art. 85 of the French Data Protection Act).

These rights can be exercised at developpement24.web@gmail.com. A reply will be sent within one month, extendable by two months for complex requests. Proof of identity may be requested in case of reasonable doubt.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07 — www.cnil.fr, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

7. Trackers and analytics

The Application uses your browser's local storage in order to function: keeping your session, saving rounds in progress and working offline. These are strictly necessary for the service you request and do not require your consent.

Analytics through Google Analytics does place trackers subject to consent. A banner lets you accept or decline it on first use. Declining does not limit the Application in any way. To change your choice, clear the site data in your browser settings.

8. Security

Exchanges between the Application and its servers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, encrypted at rest. Database access rules restrict reading and writing to the account holder alone. Subscription statuses can only be modified by the server. Payment service keys are held in a secret manager and appear in no public file.

As no system is infallible, the publisher undertakes to notify any data breach to the CNIL within 72 hours, and to the individuals concerned where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

9. Minors

The Application is not intended for people under 15 years old. In France, a minor's consent to the processing of their data in an online service is valid only from that age; below it, the authorisation of the holder of parental responsibility is required. No data is knowingly collected from a child under 15 without such authorisation.
